DEXBot2 — from fill to counter order

Fill-authoritative policy (§9.16): every full fill arms the pivot and attempts a crawl/re-anchor from the batch extremum — which the validator may refuse. Each mints exactly one counter obligation on the opposite rail, keyed by chain fill identity. Edge-pinned fills skip pivot/crawl/anchor but still owe a counter.

How to use this page / what to watch

Press Play or use Step → to walk a scenario; the ladder shows the book, the right-hand panels show the runtime state and the counter-debt ledger. Watch three things: where the boundary ends up, whether the fill's counter gets a free rail slot, and whether the debt is cleared by a confirmed op. The Phantom CREATE and Show blocked op toggles let you replay the confirmation and guard branches. The spread gauge turns amber when the book is wide and red when it is stalled.

boundary — highest BUY slot; the window anchor. BUY rail ≤ boundary, SELL rail ≥ boundary+gap+1.
gap band — the gap SPREAD slots between the rails; not a place for counters.
pivot — the last filled level and side; drives the direction guard.
batch extremum — furthest fill of the side in this batch; the re-anchor uses it, not just the last fill.
stamped op — the planned counter tagged with counterForFillKey; only its confirmation settles a debt.
no-slot / batch-cap — the counter is deferred: no free rail slot, or the per-cycle gap+1 budget is used.
CREATE — the on-chain order-placement op; a phantom CREATE returned no chain id and cannot settle a debt.
mid — a cached market price. Deliberately NOT consulted by any fill path here.
COW — copy-on-write grid: a plan is built on a working grid and swapped into master only if it commits.
recordLastFilledPrices — the pivot writer; it skips edge-pinned fills.
recordCounterDebt — the single writer of the in-memory obligation ledger.
resolveCounterQuota — selects and projects at most gap+1 counters per cycle.
counterOrdersPlacedCount — metric incremented only on a confirmed stamped op.
placed_order_in_band — validator reason a re-anchor would strand a live order in the band.
isDelayedRotationTrigger — synthetic dust-cancel flag; owes no counter.
src=partial — pivot log tag: the pivot came from a partial fill.
